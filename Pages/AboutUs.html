Our Story

A modern bakery with the spirit of a medieval market morning.

How The Cake Cart Began

The Cake Cart was born from one simple idea: desserts should feel like a story. Our founders fell in love with the lively charm of medieval town squares, where bakers greeted neighbors by name and every recipe was treated like treasure. We brought that same spirit forward, trading cobblestones for city streets, but keeping the heart of the old market alive in every bake.

Each morning, our kitchen starts before sunrise. Dough is mixed slowly, fillings are cooked in small batches, and cakes are finished by hand. We call it our "guild work" approach: patient technique, honest ingredients, and pride in the final craft. From honey-spice loaves to berry tarts, every item is made to feel both timeless and joyful.

Our Baking Guidelines

What We Promise

  • Fresh batches baked daily—never yesterday's leftovers.
  • Real ingredients first: butter, fruit, spice, and natural extracts.
  • Balanced sweetness so flavor always leads.
  • Clean, welcoming service for every guest and every occasion.

How We Work

  • Small-batch production for quality and consistency.
  • Seasonal menu updates inspired by market harvests.
  • Hand-finished decoration on signature cakes and pastries.
  • Continuous recipe testing to improve texture and flavor.
